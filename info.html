Competitive Aquascaping - An Overview

I always seem to get into niche hobbies - and aquascaping is definitely niche (in New Zealand at least)!

What does it involve? Well, an eye for detail and an aesthetic flair for sure, as you can see from the yearly winners on the home page.

For specifics though, here's a short list to get started.

Beginning

Aquasoil, CO2, light, fertilizer

Hardscape

Artfully placing rocks and driftwood - either a bold specific style, or emulate nature.

Planting

Understanding plant placement (foreground, midground, background) and growth style.

Filtration

Typically require canister filters, with suggested 10X tank volume per hour turnover.

Balance

The most difficult aspect. Striking the perfect balance between light, CO2, and nutrients takes a while to really understand, to avoid massive algae growth.

Maintenance

Trimming plants, weekly water changes, cleaning the tank
